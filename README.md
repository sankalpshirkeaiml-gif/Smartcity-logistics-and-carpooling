# 🌿 NexusMobility — Smart City Eco-Logistics & Carpooling Platform

> An enterprise-ready, production-grade web application for intelligent urban transit, carpooling, and metropolitan carbon emissions reduction. Inspired by Apple Mobility and Tesla Sustainability interfaces, featuring a clean light eco-tech theme, smooth 2D micro-lifting hover transitions, directional path indicators, an animated electric vehicle driving along transit routes, an ambient floating eco-particle canvas, the friendly **EcoSprout** mascot, and professional enterprise confirmation modals.

---

## 🌟 Visual, Interactive & UX Enhancements

1. **Clean Header**:
   - Streamlined navigation bar with essential primary controls: **"Rush-Hour Simulation"** and **"Book Eco-Ride"**.
2. **Smooth 2D Micro-Lifting Hover**:
   - Replaced heavy 3D mouse tilt with smooth 2D elevation (`transform: translateY(-4px)` with soft emerald/slate drop shadow) for comfortable, stutter-free interactions.
3. **Enhanced Spatial Map with Directional Flow & Animated Car Simulation**:
   - **Directional Path Indicators**: Active route corridors render animated directional energy pulses pointing from Origin to Destination.
   - **Animated Electric Vehicle**: An electric vehicle drives along the calculated route nodes in real-time with glowing headlight beams and headlight trails. Click **"Drive Route"** anytime to re-run the simulation.
4. **Ambient Floating Leaf & Energy Canvas**:
   - Soft, slow-floating green leaves and subtle blue electric energy nodes drifting across `#ambient-canvas`.
5. **EcoSprout Mascot Character**:
   - Illustrated friendly sprout mascot wearing aviator driving goggles and a leaf propeller cap featured in empty state containers and alerts.
6. **Enterprise Alert Modals**:
   - Polished modal dialogs for **Trip Dispatched** confirmations (with route corridor, time, fare split, CO₂ offset, and a "View on Route Map" button) and **Trip Reversal** alerts.

## 📑 4 Primary Tab Views

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [1. Eco-Route Finder & Map] [2. Commuter Hub] [3. Fleet & Dispatch] [4. Sustainability]│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Tab 1: Eco-Route Finder & Map**: 3D spatial map with directional arrows, animated vehicle drive, and dynamic commuter slider (1 to 4 passengers) calculating real-time fare splitting and emissions reduction.
2. **Tab 2: Commuter Passenger Hub**: Commuter booking form, live dispatch conveyor pipeline, and 5-slot priority standby lounge with EcoSprout mascot empty states.
3. **Tab 3: Driver Fleet & Dispatch Console**: Interactive top-down 4-seat vehicle blueprint (click seats to toggle availability), live fleet directory, active shared trips console with route compatibility badges (e.g. `96% Route Match`), and fast identity lookup.
4. **Tab 4: City Sustainability Analytics**: Comprehensive climate metrics, vehicles eliminated index, gamified eco-driver partner leaderboard with achievement badges (*"Carbon Crusader"*, *"5-Star Commute"*), and the printable eco-receipt viewer.
